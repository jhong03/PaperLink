Prepare your documents in 3 steps

1. Open your files

Open your spreadsheet on the left. Open a Word document on the right, type a new document, or choose a saved template.

2. Add the information

Click a spreadsheet cell, click where it belongs in the document, then choose Add to document. Green items stay connected: change either side and both update. Blue items are calculated from the spreadsheet.

Choose One per row to make a document for each employee or item. Use the arrows to check each one. A template remembers the document and its connections for next time.

3. Check and save

Choose Check pages to see the page layout. Then choose Save file / Print to create a Word file, PDF, or other finished file.

Save work keeps your progress here in the app. Saved work opens it again later.

More about opening files

Excel and similar spreadsheet files are supported. Word DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, and text documents can be opened. For an old Word DOC file, first save it as DOCX in Word. Complex Word layouts may look different; check the pages after opening a file.
